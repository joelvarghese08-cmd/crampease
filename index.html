🌼 Questions

Good to know

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes! Nothing to pay now — just fill the short order form and pay in cash when it arrives (the same price as paying online).

Why do you call to confirm COD orders?

A quick call or WhatsApp confirms your address and helps us avoid fake orders, so we can keep prices low for everyone. Please keep your phone handy after ordering.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 4–7 days depending on your pincode. We'll share tracking details by SMS/email.

Can I return a product?

If your product arrives damaged or wrong, email us within 2 days of delivery with photos/unboxing video and we'll help you out.

Do you deliver all over India?

We deliver to most pincodes in India. Most pincodes are covered; if yours isn't, we'll refund you in full.
